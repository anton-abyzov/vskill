# SpecWithCode true claims (checked 2026-11-03)

Use only these (SpecWithCode) in replies and posts. Re-check pricing and Founders numbers before quoting them.

## Safe to say

- **S01.** SpecWithCode is one interface over the coding agents' own native harnesses; it does not replace their agent loop, tools or sign-in. Wording: "One interface for Claude Code, Codex, GitHub Copilot and more, each in its own native harness on your computers." Source: https://spec-weave.com/code
- **S02.** Supported harnesses are Claude Code, Codex, GitHub Copilot, Gemini CLI, Grok Build, Cursor, OpenCode, Pi, Antigravity, Muse Code and other ACP agents. Wording: "Claude Code (Agent SDK), Codex (Codex app-server), GitHub Copilot and Gemini CLI (ACP Registry), Grok Build (ACP), Cursor (Cursor SDK), OpenCode (HTTP API)" Source: https://spec-weave.com/docs/studio/harnesses
- **S03.** Desktop app runs on macOS 13+ on Apple silicon, Windows 10 and 11 (x64 and arm64), and Linux (x64 and arm64), in early access. Wording: "Early access for macOS 13 or later on Apple silicon, Windows 10 and 11 (x64 and arm64), and Linux (x64 and arm64)." Source: https://spec-weave.com/code
- **S04.** Workers that join over Connect can be a Mac, a Linux machine (x64 or arm64) or a Windows 10/11 PC on x64. Wording: "A Mac, a Linux machine on x64 or arm64, or a Windows 10 or 11 PC on x64 can join as a worker over Connect." Source: https://spec-weave.com/code#machines
- **S05.** There is a free iPhone app on the App Store that signs in to the same Connect account and drives your machines. Wording: "On the iPhone, install SpecWithCode, open it and sign in with the same account. The computer appears in the iPhone's list." Source: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/specwithcode/id6824245458
- **S06.** A coordinator keeps the brief and splits work into worker threads that run side by side on any harness and machine you choose. Wording: "A coordinator that keeps the brief and splits the work. Worker threads on any harness, side by side, each on the machine you choose." Source: https://spec-weave.com/docs/studio/harnesses
- **S07.** Finished threads can leave the branch, open a PR, or open a PR and auto-merge; new projects default to auto-merge. Wording: "Leave the branch, open a PR, or open a PR and auto-merge. New projects default to auto-merge." Source: https://spec-weave.com/code
- **S08.** Usage-limit failover is a per-project rule: switch automatically, offer the fallback, or wait for the reset, and the thread keeps its context and worktree. Wording: "Under When a provider runs out, choose Switch automatically, Offer the fallback, or Wait for the reset. Continuation depends on a compatible, available provider and the policy you choose." Source: https://spec-weave.com/code#limits
- **S09.** Real capture on 30 Oct 2026: the Mac coordinator started a worker on a Linux machine, and when Claude Code hit its limit the coordinator carried on with Grok. Wording: "When Claude Code reached its limit, the coordinator carried on with Grok." Source: https://spec-weave.com/code#built-with-itself
- **S10.** Credits before fallback (Codex banked reset, Codex credits, Claude API credits) are opt-in, and SpecWithCode never buys credits. Wording: "Each is off until you turn it on, and SpecWithCode never buys credits for you." Source: https://spec-weave.com/code#limits
- **S11.** 367 PRs to SpecWithCode were opened by worker threads and merged between 15 and 29 Oct 2026, from 167 worker threads (100 Claude Code, 65 Codex, the rest Gemini CLI and Copilot). Wording: "367 pull requests to SpecWithCode opened by worker threads and merged, 15 to 29 October 2026" Source: https://spec-weave.com/code#built-with-itself
- **S12.** Named example PRs and the agent behind each: #880, #873, #837 Claude Code; #927, #961 Codex; #954 Gemini CLI. Wording: "#954 Skills in this session popover in the composer (Gemini CLI); #927 Prefer Connect fleet routes and support unlimited account capacity (Codex)" Source: https://spec-weave.com/code#built-with-itself
- **S13.** The app is free for every developer and team, and there is no markup on your AI subscriptions. Wording: "Free for every developer and team. Agents run on the Claude, Codex, Gemini or Cursor plans you already pay for. We never resell tokens." Source: https://spec-weave.com/pricing
- **S14.** Free includes 3 Connect machines plus your phone, and local network and SSH routes are never counted. Wording: "Connect: reach 3 machines from anywhere, and your phone. Local network and SSH routes, never counted." Source: https://spec-weave.com/pricing
- **S15.** Founders: the first 300 accounts to link a machine through Connect get 10 machines, a founder number, and a seat that never expires; there is no form and no card. Wording: "The first 300 accounts to link a machine through Connect. No form: the seat is granted on that link." Source: https://spec-weave.com/pricing
- **S16.** Pro costs $20 a month per account and is a waitlist; it adds unlimited Connect machines and hosted private skills, and nobody is charged before it opens. Wording: "Pro will be billed by card in your SpecWithCode account when it opens; nobody on the waitlist is charged before then." Source: https://spec-weave.com/pricing
- **S17.** Teams get an annual contract for 2-10 people, and there is a $990 two-week founding-team pilot with five places, free if the handoff drill fails. Wording: "If the handoff drill fails in your repo, you do not pay." Source: https://spec-weave.com/pricing
- **S18.** Connect gives each linked machine a private relay address, with no router changes, so other Macs, a Linux server, a browser or an iPhone can reach it from anywhere. Wording: "Each machine you link gets a private address on a hosted relay. Nothing on your router changes and nothing is exposed to the public internet without your sign-in." Source: https://spec-weave.com/docs/studio/connect
- **S19.** One coordinator can start workers on other machines you have added and allowed; nothing is copied between machines. Wording: "Threads run on the coordinator's machine unless the project or the coordinator names another machine you allowed." Source: https://spec-weave.com/code#machines
- **S20.** Adding machines adds capacity only through each machine's own accounts; two machines on the same account share its limits. Wording: "Two machines signed in to the same account share that account's limits, so running agents on both spends them faster, not slower." Source: https://spec-weave.com/code#machines
- **S21.** Skills can follow you across harnesses because the Verified Skill / vskill CLI installs the same skill into each agent it finds. Wording: "Most harnesses read skills from their own folders ... vskill install can add the same skill to each agent it detects." Source: https://spec-weave.com/docs/studio/harnesses
- **S22.** Agents can use your Chrome through the SpecWithCode Browser extension, and on macOS can control the desktop with permission. Wording: "Native desktop control is macOS only and requires permission on that Mac." Source: https://spec-weave.com/code#reach

## Avoid

- **A01.** Say 'source available', not 'open source'. Wording: "AVOID: 'open source' / 'MIT' / 'OSS'. USE: 'Free, source available'" Source: https://spec-weave.com/code
- **A02.** Don't say 'no account needed'. Say the app needs no account with us; Connect and founder seats need a sign-in. Wording: "AVOID: 'no account', 'no sign-up'. USE: 'The app needs no account with us.'" Source: https://spec-weave.com/pricing
- **A03.** Never describe where the code came from or what it derives from, and don't compare it with other agent apps by name. Wording: "USE: 'SpecWithCode is its own product: a coordinator, threads, Connect, usage failover, skills.'"
- **A04.** Don't say everything stays local or runs offline. Wording: "AVOID: 'your code never leaves your machine', 'local AI'. USE: 'Local execution does not mean offline AI.'" Source: https://spec-weave.com/code
- **A05.** Don't claim savings or that subscriptions replace API spend. Wording: "AVOID: 'save X%', 'replaces your API bill'. USE: 'Measure your own workload before claiming savings.'" Source: https://spec-weave.com/code
- **A06.** Don't call SpecWithCode a harness or agent, or say it ships its own model. Wording: "AVOID: 'our agent', 'a new coding agent'. USE: 'SpecWithCode is an interface. It runs no agent loop of its own.'" Source: https://spec-weave.com/docs/studio/harnesses
- **A07.** Don't claim unlimited machines on Free or Founders, or 'one goal, one PR' as a guarantee. Wording: "AVOID: 'unlimited machines free', 'always one PR'. USE: 'Free: 3 Connect machines; Founders: 10; Pro (waitlist): unlimited.'" Source: https://spec-weave.com/pricing
- **A08.** Don't call failover unique or say it works with every provider. Wording: "AVOID: 'the only tool that fails over', 'never hit a limit again'. USE: 'Continuation depends on a compatible, available provider and the policy you choose.'" Source: https://spec-weave.com/code#limits
- **A09.** Don't call the Windows/Linux builds signed or the product GA. Wording: "AVOID: 'production-ready', 'signed on all platforms'. USE: 'early access'; 'Mac releases are signed and notarized; Windows and Linux installers are unsigned.'" Source: https://spec-weave.com/code
